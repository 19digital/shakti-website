ABOUT SHAKTI

Built in the Workshop, Proven on the Mill Floor

Founded in Bardhaman, West Bengal, Shakti Engineering Works has spent over 20 years designing and fabricating the pre-milling machinery that modern rice mills across India, Bangladesh, Myanmar and Sri Lanka run on every day.

Our Story

From a Small Fabrication Unit to a Pan-India Manufacturer

What began as a small fabrication workshop serving local rice mills has grown into a full pre-milling machinery manufacturer — every elevator, dryer, parboiling plant and silo engineered, fabricated and quality-checked in-house.

Today Shakti Engineering Works equips more than 500 mills, with installations pan-India and exports to Bangladesh, Myanmar and Sri Lanka, backed by ISO 9001:2015 certified quality systems.
